Public Data Sources

  • Tidy Tuesday is an R organization that posts a new public data set every week, and has since 2018. You can find the data, but there are also people who livestream analyzing the data, and those videos are on YouTube and are an excellent way to learn how to use the tidyverse set of packages (which include ggplot2)

  • Utah GIS Data - maps, landmarks, demographics, climate, economy, energy, farming, geoscience, health, history, political science, recreation, society, transportation, utilities, and water.

  • Data.gov - more than 500k datasets from the US government

    • Lake Powell Releases and Reservoir Elevation

    • Great Salt Lake Bathymetry: Elevation, Area, and Volume tables

    • EnviroAtlas Salt Lake City

      • 1 \(\text{km}^2\) resolution data on the proportion of impervious surfaces
      • Tree cover along walkable roads and busy roads

    • Supply Chain Greenhouse Gas Emission Factors

    • Meteorite Landings

    • CDC WONDER: Cancer Statistics

    • Baby Names from the Social Security Administration
